Complete all twelve labours in order. Desktop: ←/→ move, ↑ jumps on platform levels, arrow keys move in top-down levels, Enter = Action. WASD also works. Mobile: controls change automatically for each labour; landscape is recommended but portrait also works. Skip is available on all devices; using it disables the diploma for that run.
A playful arcade interpretation of the conventional Twelve Labours. Mythic details vary across ancient and later traditions.
